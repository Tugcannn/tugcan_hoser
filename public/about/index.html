Who I am

I’m Tuğcan, originally from Istanbul, Turkey, and now based in Munich. I studied Computer Engineering at Özyeğin University before moving to Germany to pursue a master’s in Informatics at the Technical University of Munich, specializing in machine learning.

My work has taken me from hospitals to molecules, retail shelves and music. Along the way, I’ve never minded being the person who knows the least in the room, there’s always something new to learn.

Today, I’m the co-founder and CTO of Song Go, an AI music platform with 1,500+ monthly active users. In a short time, we’ve reached people from Arizona to the Philippines, and from Hungary to Botswana. Hearing what they’ve created and reading their messages has been one of the most rewarding parts of building it.

Beyond work, I’ve traveled to many countries, lived in different cities and met people who have changed how I see the world. I’m grateful to those who have broadened my perspective and helped shape who I am today. I also love building things and starting new ventures, which probably explains why my quiet weekends rarely stay quiet.

Basketball has been a big part of my life, too. I played competitively as a licensed player for 14 years, and that competitive spirit has stayed with me: I’ll fight for every loose ball, every stubborn bug and every last percentage point of accuracy.

Naturally, The Last Dance is a favorite. Few documentaries leave me as motivated and inspired, which explains the photo with His Airness, Michael Jordan. We may not have shared a court, but at least we’ve shared a wall.
